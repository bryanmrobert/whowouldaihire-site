What are the best AI visibility tools for small businesses?

There are two kinds: trackers (they monitor whether AI mentions you) and services (they fix why it doesn't). Most trackers are built for national brands; local businesses need city-specific questions and fixes to local sources such as directories and Google Business Profile.

When comparing tools, check: whether questions include your city, how many times each question is repeated (a single run is noise (Gumshoe)), which assistants are covered, and whether you get a fix list rather than just a chart.

WhoWouldAIHire combines both for local businesses: a free measured report, then an optional done-for-you fix with re-measurement.
