What are the best agencies for getting local businesses into AI answers?

Judge any agency on three things: do they measure across several assistants with repeated questions, do they fix local sources (not just your website), and do they show a before/after with the same method?

Red flags: guaranteed #1 rankings in ChatGPT (no one controls AI answers), proof based on one screenshot, or selling llms.txt as the main fix (it's rarely read (Ahrefs)). Fake reviews are illegal under the FTC's rule (16 CFR 465).

WhoWouldAIHire measures before and after with the same questions, and focuses on the factors with the most evidence behind them.
