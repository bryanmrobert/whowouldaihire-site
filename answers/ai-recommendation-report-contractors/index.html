What is an AI recommendation report for contractors?

A report showing which contractors AI assistants recommend in your city for your trade, how often you're named, and what to fix. WhoWouldAIHire's version is free.

It includes your visibility score (the share of answers naming you, averaged across assistants), the competitors named instead, the sources the assistants cited, where you appear in the answer, and an impact/effort action plan. See a sample leaderboard (fictional businesses).
