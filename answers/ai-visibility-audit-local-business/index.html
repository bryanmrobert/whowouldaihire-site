What is an AI visibility audit for a local business?

An AI visibility audit asks AI assistants the questions your customers ask (for example, 'best plumber near me in Richmond'), many times, and measures how often you're named, who's named instead, and which sources the assistants relied on.

Single checks are misleading because AI answers change almost every time: in a 2,961-run study, the same list of brands came back less than 1% of the time (SparkToro). Visibility rates over many runs are stable enough to track (Gumshoe).

Our free report asks 18 real customer questions, 3 times each, across several assistants with web search turned on, and shows your mention rate, the competitors named, the sources cited, and a prioritized fix list. Published audit prices range from $49 to $3,500 (median about $300) (Peekl). Ours is free.
