Questions

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. Nobody controls AI answers. A report is a snapshot of what the assistants said when we measured. We don't promise a ranking, leads, or revenue.

How much of my time does it take?

The free score is a short form. Checkout asks for the email where we should send what you bought. We never need your passwords.

How do I pay?

Paid products use Stripe checkout. You pay on Stripe's page. We email the deliverable to the address you used there, within 2 business days. The free score does not ask for a card. Nothing is charged by email.

Can I cancel the monthly re-check?

Yes, anytime. Cancellation stops the next renewal. You keep the period you've already paid for.

When is the map and action plan the right purchase?

After a report, when you want a written plan for what to do next. It is a consultative add-on. We don't offer it as the first step, and a score of 0 is not a reason to buy it.
