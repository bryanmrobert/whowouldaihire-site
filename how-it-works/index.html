How it works

How we measure and fix AI visibility

No screenshots, no guesswork. We measure the same way every time, so changes are real.

1. The measurement

We take 18 questions real customers ask for your trade and city ("best emergency plumber in Richmond", "who replaces water heaters near me"), and ask each one 3 times on each AI assistant through its official API with web search turned on. That's 54 answers per assistant.

Your visibility score is the share of answers that name you, averaged across assistants. We also record who's named instead, where you appear in each answer, and which sources were cited. Running each question several times matters: individual AI answers almost never repeat, but rates measured over many runs are stable (SparkToro, Gumshoe).

2. The fix

  1. A page for every service you sell
    Plain-language pages that answer the questions customers actually ask. Topical relevance on your own site is the strongest measured correlate of being cited. Source
  2. Be listed where each assistant looks
    ChatGPT leans on directories such as BBB, Expertise.com and Angi plus Yelp data; Claude cites Yelp heavily; Google's AI leans on your Business Profile. Source
  3. Show up on 'best of' lists
    Expert-curated best-of lists ranked as the #1 AI visibility factor in Whitespark's 2026 expert survey. Source
  4. A complete Google Business Profile with steady reviews
    Profile completeness, review count and owner replies all correlate positively with being cited. Source
  5. Don't block AI crawlers
    If robots.txt blocks OpenAI, Anthropic or Perplexity's search bots, they can't read you. It's a one-line fix. Source

Setup takes one short guided session with Clara, our assistant. You add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile and invite us to your website. We never ask for passwords. After that, we do the work.

3. The proof

We repeat the exact same measurement at day 30, 60 and 90 and show you the before and after. Some changes appear within days (new pages can be crawled within hours). Others take weeks: directory listings typically take up to about 5–6 weeks to go live (BrightLocal).

What we won't do

  • Promise a #1 spot in ChatGPT. Nobody controls AI answers.
  • Write fake reviews or filter which customers get asked. That's illegal (FTC rule) and it backfires.
  • Ask for your passwords.
