How it works

Measure. Fix. Prove.

The same method every time, so any change is real.

01 · Measure

We ask the questions your customers ask.

18 real questions for your trade and city, 3 times each, on each AI assistant with web search on. Single AI answers rarely repeat, so we measure rates, not screenshots (SparkToro).

  • Your visibility score
  • Who's named instead
  • Sources AI cited
  • What to fix first
02 · Fix

We fix the sources AI relies on.

One guided setup of about 15 minutes. You add us as a manager, never share a password, and we do the rest within 14 days.

  • A page for every service you sell
  • Be listed where each assistant looks
  • Show up on 'best of' lists
  • A complete Google Business Profile with steady reviews
  • Don't block AI crawlers
03 · Prove

We re-measure and show you.

The exact same questions at day 30, 60 and 90. Pages can be read within days; directory listings can take about 5–6 weeks (BrightLocal).

What we won't do
  • Promise a #1 spot. Nobody controls AI answers.
  • Write fake reviews (FTC rule)
  • Ask for your passwords
